---
"better-auth-dbsc": patch
---

Expire a session's bound cookie and unbound marker whenever a response expires a cookie that holds that session, not only on `/sign-out`. This covers Better Auth's session cookie (`/sign-out`, `/revoke-session` and `/revoke-sessions` on the current session, `/delete-user` and its emailed link, `/get-session` finding the session gone) and `multiSession()`'s `_multi-` cookies (`/multi-session/revoke`, `/multi-session/set-active` on a session that is gone, signing in again to the same account, and every account on `/sign-out`). The plugin reads this from the response, without a database read, and the last Set-Cookie for a name counts. When a response signs the browser in to another session, the plugin reads the old session once and expires its DBSC cookies if it is gone (`/change-password` with `revokeOtherSessions`); admin impersonation and switching accounts keep them. With `crossDomain()` from `@convex-dev/better-auth`, cookies already moved to `Set-Better-Auth-Cookie` count too, before the remaining Set-Cookie headers. Chrome ends its device-bound session on the next request. A sign-in held by `twoFactor()` expires the session cookie the browser had, so its DBSC cookies go too. An unbound marker is no longer handed out for a session that the same response ends. On runtimes without `Headers.getSetCookie`, the plugin falls back to the joined `Set-Cookie` header, as Better Auth does.
